The model

Small by design.
Built to last.

01

Transparent registration

Every approved assignment will have a reviewable JSON record and change history, managed through GitHub.

02

Independent operation

Registrants may delegate their names to independent DNS providers, rather than depending on WNIC to host every record.

03

Institutional continuity

Names identifying an office remain with the office. Administrative custody can change without breaking the institutional address.

Practical questions

A namespace, not a new TLD.

WNIC will allocate subdomains beneath westarctica.net. These are not ICANN-accredited domain registrations, and they do not convey ownership of the parent domain. Public registration policies, eligibility, and a technical publication process must be approved before applications open.
